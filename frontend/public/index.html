    About

    How Mahindi works

    The model

    A neural network trained only on healthy maize leaves. The further your leaf is from what it learned, the higher the damage level.

    Your privacy

    Photos are analysed in memory and deleted straight away. Nothing is stored and no account is needed.

    The advice

    Summarised from CIMMYT, NARO and ILRI research in East Africa, and under review with an agricultural extension officer.
